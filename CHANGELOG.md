# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-28

- Package 102 unchanged payment SVGs in six styles as a standalone Rails engine.
- Support conventional Bundler loading and namespaced Rails asset paths.
- Add automatic `payment_icon_tag` and `payment_icon_path` view helpers with
  variant selection, normal Rails options, and validated icon names.
- Verify production asset delivery from the built gem with Propshaft and Sprockets.
- Add pinned upstream import, byte verification, and license notice preservation.
- Preserve Apache-2.0 licensing and credit Aaron Fagan and contributors for artwork.
